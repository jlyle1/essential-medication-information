### Essential Medication Information Use Cases

This IG is designed to support sharing of patient medication information with consistent assumptions around what information is essential. It is based on the VA's Essential Medication Information policy, which informs templates and After Visit Summaries supported by CPRS.

#### VA Provider seeks VA History
This case is supported by current technology using CPRS. The VA Provider views the medication list in CPRS, which presents the information defined by the policy.

#### VA Provider seeks History from External Provider 
In this case, the VA provider requests medication information from a community provider. This IG provides guidance on how to request and provide information that fulfills the policy.

#### External Provider seeks History from VA or External Provider 
A provider who may have trained at the VA understands and prefers the data set as defined by VA policy. The IG provides guidance on how to request and, for external data sources, how to provide information that fulfills the policy.

#### Provider seeks History from Provider network (future)
A provider requests medication information from a community provider. The community provider is aware of other providers that may have additional or more current information 
* and forwards the request to the additional source(s) with instruction to respond to the requester
* and forwards the request to the additional source(s) with instruction to respond to the intermediary, who will consolidate the response
* and informs the requester of the additional sources
